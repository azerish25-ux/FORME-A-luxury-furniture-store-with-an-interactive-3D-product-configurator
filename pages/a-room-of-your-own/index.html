THE FORME POINT OF VIEW

A softer kind of Sunday.

There is a particular pleasure in a room with nowhere to be. A book left open. A coffee that takes its time. Light making its way across a familiar floor.

Leave a little space

A generous room is not always a large one. Start with the pieces you use every day, and leave room for the ways you move between them. A low table keeps sight lines open. A chair turned towards the sofa makes conversation the centre of things.

Keep it personal

A room becomes yours through use. A favourite cup, a stack of books, a throw within reach. The best pieces leave room for these small signs of living.
